Electrical services for every kind of project.

Residential

Bring your home up to code, modernize your wiring, and add the lighting and EV power your family needs.

  • EV charger installation (Tesla, Level 2)
  • Pot lights, fixtures & smart switches
  • Knob & tube / aluminum re-wiring
  • Generator transfer switches
  • Hot tub & pool wiring
  • Renovations, additions & basements
  • Troubleshooting & emergency repairs

How it works

Four simple steps from call to commissioning.

  1. 01

    Call or message

    Tell us what's going on. We answer day or night and ask the right questions to scope the work fast.

  2. 02

    Site visit & written quote

    We come out, look at the job, and send you an itemized fixed-price quote — usually within 24 hours.

  3. 03
